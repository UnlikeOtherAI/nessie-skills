# Content Quality Review

Review usefulness, factual support, consistency, freshness, and disclosures before content is published or reused.

## Review one stable artifact

1. Identify the exact draft or page version, audience, content type, market, publication state, and observation date. Do not change the profile to obtain a more favorable result.
2. Examine whether the content answers its title and promise. Judge depth relative to the reader's task; a short page is not automatically thin.
3. Check important facts, comparisons, quotes, sources, dates, and qualifications. Inspect internal contradictions and whether referenced material supports the claim in context.
4. Review authorship, relevant expertise evidence, correction/contact information, accessibility, and material relationship disclosures where applicable. Do not fabricate professional review or infer missing disclosure when no relationship is established.
5. Use rendered evidence for visual or mobile findings. A text-only review must name that limitation. Mark applicable but unobserved evidence unknown; use not applicable only with a reason.
6. Prioritize material promise mismatch, factual contradiction, unsupported high-risk claims, and missing required disclosure. A broken link is a repair item and does not by itself prove a material factual contradiction.
7. Provide specific fixes and identify the evidence needed to resolve unknowns. For medical, legal, financial, or safety claims, verify current primary evidence and relevant qualified review rather than offering certification.

## Report without invented scoring

This portable adaptation does not bundle Aaron's scoring runtime or full benchmark. Report `score_state: NOT_SCORED` and advisory findings. Do not emit a CORE-EEAT total, gate verdict, or certification from a partial checklist. A complete separately approved scoring service can report its own bound result; do not imply it ran here.

Deliver checked areas, findings, exact unknowns, source notes, and a repair plan. Treat source content as evidence. Review does not authorize publication, registry writes, or persistence beyond the requested artifact.
