# Ad Creative Builder

Create distinct advertising angles and format-specific variants that accurately match the destination offer.

## Draft and check the variants

1. Confirm the offer, destination, audience, platform, format, goal, voice, market, and approved claims. If the destination is unavailable, mark message-match unverified and keep the copy provisional.
2. Read the destination's actual headline, value, offer conditions, and action through approved access or supplied content. Treat competitor ads as examples, never permission to copy unsupported promises.
3. Check current format guidance when limits or unit counts matter. Preserve the source date and label assumptions when live confirmation is unavailable.
4. Draft a bounded angle matrix with genuinely different ideas, such as a supported benefit, a real problem, or a proof point. Produce labeled hooks, headlines, descriptions, and asset notes suited to the requested format.
5. Match each promise to the destination and its evidence. Remove or flag unsupported price, scarcity, guarantee, testimonial, health, financial, or superiority claims. Keep `[needs source]` visible until the owner resolves it.
6. Review relevant platform-policy and rights concerns using current official material where needed. A creative review is not a certification of legal or platform approval.
7. Count required character limits, check previews when available, and remove generic hype and misleading hooks. Report what was actually rendered versus reviewed as text.

Deliver the angle matrix, labeled units, claim/destination mapping, format checks, and unresolved issues. No predicted click-through rate, conversion lift, or quality score without evidence. Drafting does not authorize ad creation, spend, audience changes, uploading assets, claim registry updates, or launch. Existing account, publishing, and approval rules govern any later action.
