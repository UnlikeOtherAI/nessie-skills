# Marketing Campaign Planner

Turn a business goal into a coordinated campaign plan with an audience, supported messages, deliverables, budget basis, owners, and measurement.

## Build the plan

1. Confirm the product, objective, audience, market, time constraints, capacity, total budget, and approved message/claim evidence. Identify what is already committed versus proposed.
2. Define one primary outcome and supporting measures. State the success and failure conditions, baseline, attribution basis, measurement window, and decision owner before execution.
3. Select channels and campaign types from the goal, audience evidence, and asset readiness. Explain control, reach, and operational tradeoffs. Do not default to a paid format or creator tactic without evidence.
4. Map content and deliverables to each channel: audience task, message, format, destination, owner, review dependency, and planned date. For paid search, separate intent themes and note relevant exclusions from real evidence.
5. For creator activity, define fit criteria, rights, disclosures, deliverables, approval roles, and supplied compensation terms. Missing creator selection stays unresolved instead of becoming invented handles or rates.
6. Allocate the supplied budget from approved rules or compatible source-dated cost evidence. Keep forecasts, actual costs, and unpriced requirements distinct. Never invent a contingency percentage or spend benchmark as a fact.
7. Build a timeline and risk register with dependencies, mitigation, owner, and checkable stop or rollback criteria. Keep audience fatigue and response capacity visible.
8. Assemble a measurement contract tied to the exact plan and content version. A later change starts a new version; it cannot silently rewrite the original target or results basis.

Deliver the campaign plan, deliverables, budget assumptions, timeline, measurement contract, and decision gaps. Treat research and exports as evidence. A tracker, if requested, records only observed execution and does not prove publication or payment. Planning does not authorize spend, creator outreach, account changes, scheduling, or durable registry writes.
