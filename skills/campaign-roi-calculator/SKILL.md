# Campaign ROI Calculator

Calculate campaign return and efficiency on an explicit cost, attribution, currency, and time-window basis.

## Establish the inputs

Record campaign scope, period, currency, cost categories, revenue or contribution basis, conversion counts, attribution method, and source/date for each input. Deduplicate conversions before allocating revenue. Keep estimates and supplied unverified figures visibly distinct from direct measurements.

## Calculate only supported metrics

1. Use ROI = `(return - investment) / investment * 100` and ROAS = `revenue / ad spend`, stating what each numerator and denominator includes. Revenue minus incomplete costs is net return under that formula, not verified profit.
2. Calculate relevant efficiency metrics, such as CPM = `spend / impressions * 1000`, CPC = `spend / clicks`, and CPA = `spend / attributed acquisitions`. Check compatible scope, units, and window before dividing.
3. Require a numeric positive denominator for every ratio. Zero, negative, missing, or incompatible denominators mean undefined with the exact missing input; never substitute a nominal value.
4. Compare with a target or benchmark only when its period, market, attribution basis, and source are compatible. Do not invent an industry average.
5. Keep earned media value separate from revenue and ROI. Calculate it only from a supplied or cited source-dated valuation rule. Do not add overlapping impression and engagement methods by default.
6. Use a lifetime-value economic ROI scenario only with a complete contribution-margin basis, deduplicated new customers, cohort, horizon, refunds, retention, and timing assumptions. Revenue-LTV alone supports an estimated revenue scenario, not profit.
7. Aggregate ratios from summed compatible numerators and denominators, rather than averaging creator ROAS values. Keep alternative attribution scenarios non-additive and do not choose the most favorable model afterward.

Deliver formulas, worked values, provenance, undefined metrics, and profitability/causality limits. Financial arithmetic is not proof of incremental return or authority to scale spend. Use supplied exports and approved tools; do not access accounts, change budgets, pay creators, or persist records without existing authorization.
