# Launch Retrospective

Compare a launch's actual execution and outcomes with its original plan to identify supported lessons and next changes.

## Review the launch

1. Bind the launch plan, content versions, actual action evidence, measurement contract, and review period. A live URL or planned row alone does not establish that every required action happened.
2. Recover targets and baselines declared before launch. Mark a reconstructed target as reconstructed; never retrofit it as a prior commitment.
3. Build actual-versus-target rows by channel or workstream with compatible units, windows, attribution, and evidence. Keep platform self-reports separate from owned analytics when they differ.
4. Identify the most consequential miss or unexpected success. Trace an evidence-backed explanation and test alternative causes. A repeated "why" question cannot turn a plausible platform story into a confirmed cause.
5. Compare results with capacity, readiness, message, and execution evidence. Missing receipts or an incomplete measurement window keep conclusions provisional.
6. Recommend keep, stop, or change actions according to the agreed decision rule and owner. If those are absent, give supported observations and explicitly unresolved decisions.
7. Write a small set of useful changes for the next launch, each with rationale, owner proposal, and check. Preserve factual uncertainty and avoid carrying unsupported claims forward as learning.

Deliver the outcome table, execution gaps, cause evidence, recommendations, and the next decision. Use supplied artifacts and approved reads. Missing scoring runtime means `NOT_SCORED`; a retrospective is not a launch-certification gate. It does not authorize changing live campaign records, reopening work, contacting customers, starting a momentum campaign, scheduling checks, or deploying a correction.
