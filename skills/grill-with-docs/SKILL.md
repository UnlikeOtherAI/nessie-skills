# Grill / Grill with Docs

Stress-test a plan by resolving the assumptions and decisions that could change what gets built or done.

## Use the known context first

Read the supplied plan, constraints, previous decisions, and relevant permitted evidence. Separate facts you can investigate from preferences only a person can decide. A missing fact is a research task, not automatically a question for the user.

## Work through the decisions

1. Name the goal and the outcome that would count as success. Identify the decisions on which the rest of the plan depends.
2. Map those dependencies. Ask about decisions whose prerequisites are already settled before asking about their consequences.
3. Ask a small round of questions that materially change the work. Explain the tradeoff and offer a recommendation with its reason. Make it easy to disagree; do not phrase a question so agreement is presumed.
4. Test each proposed answer against a concrete ordinary case and a failure case. Check capacity, ownership, reversibility, access, and the user's actual workflow where relevant.
5. Record what was settled, what remains open, and which assumption needs evidence. Do not supply the person's answer on their behalf. Continue independent investigation while a required decision is pending.
6. Stop when the plan is actionable and the remaining uncertainty is explicit. Further interviewing needs a decision it will resolve.

## Deliver

Return the revised plan, the important decisions and reasons, open questions, and completion criteria. In documentation mode, propose updates to the existing plan, glossary, or decision record. Write them only within the task's authorized scope and existing document conventions. Record a separate decision note only when a real tradeoff is costly to reverse and a future reader needs the reason. This skill does not require creating documents or tracker issues after every exchange.
