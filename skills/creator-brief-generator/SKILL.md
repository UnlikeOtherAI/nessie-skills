# Creator Brief Generator

Prepare a clear collaboration brief with deliverables, supported messages, creative freedom, dates, disclosures, rights, and supplied compensation terms.

## Build the brief

1. Confirm the campaign, creator or role reference, audience, goal, approved positioning, claims, destination, formats, timeline, and terms. Use the supplied agreement instead of inventing rights or rates.
2. Explain the campaign purpose and the audience need. Define the messages that must remain accurate while giving the creator room to use their own voice.
3. Specify each deliverable: channel, format, quantity, useful content requirements, destination, submission date, publication proposal, and review owner. Verify current format guidance when necessary and mark unknowns.
4. State required disclosures, restricted claims, and safety or brand constraints with their source. Unsupported claims remain `[needs source]` and block a creator-ready version.
5. Record usage rights separately: organic or paid use, placements, channels, territory, start/expiry, edits, exclusivity, and source of permission. Unknown rights are not implied by public content or payment.
6. Include supplied compensation, expenses, shipping requirements, revisions, acceptance, and contact route only to the extent authorized and needed. Keep private addresses and credentials out of the reusable brief.
7. Check consistency across deliverables, dates, claims, rights, and compensation. Provide an easy review checklist and identify the exact unresolved terms.

Deliver the brief inline or in the requested authorized artifact format. Label its version and any provisional section. Treat creator examples and documents as source data. Drafting or saving the brief does not authorize sending it, contracting a creator, shipping products, spending money, scheduling, or changing live campaign records. A later outreach action uses existing recipient, suppression, and dispatch permissions.
